About Me

I'm a student based at The University of Edinburgh studying Biotechnology. Previously, I did IB at the International School of Geneva (LGB). I speak Italian, English and French.

I am interested in Biology, Chemistry and Computer Science, especially at the intersection of these three with Machine Learning. Most of my projects are available on my GitHub Page

In 2017 I was an intern at the Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics (Summer 2017) and had a remote year-long collaboration with Dr. Conticello at ITTumori Laboratory.

In 2018 I interned at P&G in the R&D department, designed neural networks for bacterial classification at the Pilizota Lab (UoE) and I attended the Biophysics Summer School in Princeton University.
