About Me

I'm a student based at The University of Edinburgh studying Biochemistry. Previously, I studied in the International School of Geneva (La Grande Boissière), thus I speak Italian, English and French.

I am genuinely interested in all fields of science, especially Biology, Chemistry and Computer Science. My aim is to find alternative ways to represent and filter data to find hidden patterns.

I was an intern at the Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics (Summer 2017) and have an ongoing collaboration with Dr. Conticello in ITTumori Laboratory.

Interests

Representing data collates my passions for science, graphic design and computer science.

As part of my training at the Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, I produced various types of graphs mainly using JavaScript. The main libraries I used were D3.js and Viz.js. Subsequently, the graphs were converted in Polymer Elements.

These graphs were produced respectively for the Proteome Informatics Group and the CALIPHO Group at the Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics.

I also used Python to perform statistical analysis of DNA from conotoxins at the Istituto Toscano Tumori (ITT) of which results are currently being published. These also involved the production of visual representations of data.
